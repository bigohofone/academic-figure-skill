# Task: t-sne

Draw a scatter plot of a 2-D embedding from `t-sne.json`, for an ICLR paper (single-column layout, 5.5 in text width).

Data: `class_1` to `class_10`: t-SNE coordinates (`x`, `y`) of 100 CIFAR-10 test samples per class, from the penultimate-layer features of a ResNet-34. The classes have no names.

Deliverable: the figure in the figures folder with its caption, as `t-sne.png` and `t-sne.pdf`.
