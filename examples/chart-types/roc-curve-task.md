# Task: roc-curve

Draw the ROC curves of three toxicity classifiers on the test set (ICML, single column). `roc-curve.json` maps each method to its curve: `fpr` (false positive rate) and `tpr` (true positive rate). The point of the figure: our classifier dominates both baselines at every operating point.

The data is synthetic, made to look like typical results. Deliverable: the figure in `figures/figures.ipynb` with its caption, as `roc-curve.png` and `roc-curve.pdf`.
