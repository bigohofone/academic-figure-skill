# Task: reward-distribution

Draw the distribution of reward-model scores of responses from three policies (ICML, single column). `reward-distribution.json`: `rewards[policy]`, the scores of 400 responses each. The point: the scores are bimodal (bad and good responses), and our policy moves most responses into the high-reward mode.

The data is synthetic, made to look like typical results. Deliverable: the figure in `figures/figures.ipynb` with its caption, as `reward-distribution.png` and `reward-distribution.pdf`.
