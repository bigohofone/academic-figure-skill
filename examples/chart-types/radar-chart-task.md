# Task: radar-chart

Draw the scores of three 8–9B models across six benchmarks (for an ICLR paper). `radar-chart.json`: `benchmarks`, and `scores[model]` (accuracy %, same order). The point: ours has the most balanced profile, with its biggest gains on GSM8K and MATH.

The data is synthetic, made to look like typical results. Venue: ICLR (single-column layout, 5.5 in text width). Size the figure for it, whatever the original paper's layout was.

Deliverable: the figure in `figures/figures.ipynb` with its caption, as `radar-chart.png` and `radar-chart.pdf`.
