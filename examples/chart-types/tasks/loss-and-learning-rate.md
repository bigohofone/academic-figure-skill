# Task: loss-and-learning-rate

Draw a line chart of the training loss and the learning rate over training steps, in one panel, from `loss-and-learning-rate.json`, for an ICLR paper.

Data: `steps`, `training_loss` (cross-entropy) and `learning_rate` (linear warmup, then cosine decay), one value per step.

Deliverable: the figure in the figures folder with its caption, as `loss-and-learning-rate.png` and `loss-and-learning-rate.pdf`.
