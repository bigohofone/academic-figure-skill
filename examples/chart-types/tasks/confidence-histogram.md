# Task: confidence-histogram

Draw the distribution of our model's confidence on correct and incorrect answers (for an ICLR paper). `confidence-histogram.json`: `correct` and `incorrect`, the confidence (0–1) of each answer. The point: correct answers are mostly very confident, while incorrect ones spread across the range, so confidence separates them.

The data is synthetic, made to look like typical results. Venue: ICLR (single-column layout, 5.5 in text width). Size the figure for it, whatever the original paper's layout was.

Deliverable: the figure in the figures folder with its caption, as `confidence-histogram.png` and `confidence-histogram.pdf`.
