# Task: data-mixture

Draw a stacked area chart from `data-mixture.json`, for an ICLR paper.

Data: `steps` (training steps), and `shares[domain]`: the fraction of pretraining tokens from each domain at each step (the domains sum to 1). The mixture changes in stages, at 30k and 70k steps.

Deliverable: the figure in the figures folder with its caption, as `data-mixture.png` and `data-mixture.pdf`.
