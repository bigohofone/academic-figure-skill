# Task: correlation

Draw our reward model's score against the human rating (1–7 Likert, averaged over annotators) for 300 responses (for an ICLR paper). `correlation.json`: `human_rating` and `reward_score`, one value per response. The point: the reward model's scores correlate with human ratings.

The data is synthetic, made to look like a real experiment. Venue: ICLR (single-column layout, 5.5 in text width). Size the figure for it, whatever the original paper's layout was.

Deliverable: the figure in the figures folder with its caption, as `correlation.png` and `correlation.pdf`.
