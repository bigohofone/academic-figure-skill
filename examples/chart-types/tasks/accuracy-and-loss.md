# Task: accuracy-and-loss

Draw line charts, one panel per metric and dataset, side by side, from `accuracy-and-loss.json`, for an ICLR paper.

Data: `epochs`, `validation_accuracy[dataset][method]` (0–1) and `training_loss[dataset][method]` (cross-entropy) for two datasets (CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100) and two methods (Ours, Baseline), one value per epoch.

Deliverable: the figure in the figures folder with its caption, as `accuracy-and-loss.png` and `accuracy-and-loss.pdf`.
