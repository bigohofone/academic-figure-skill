# Task: small-multiples

Draw line charts, one panel per dataset, side by side, from `small-multiples.json`, for an ICML paper, as a figure spanning both columns (`figure*`).

Data: `epochs`, and `accuracy[dataset][method]` for three datasets (CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, Tiny-ImageNet) and three methods (Ours, Mixup, Baseline): 3 runs (seeds) each, one validation accuracy (0–1) per epoch.

Deliverable: the figure in the figures folder with its caption, as `small-multiples.png` and `small-multiples.pdf`.
