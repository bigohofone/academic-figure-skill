# Task: hyperparameter-grid

Draw a heatmap from `hyperparameter-grid.json`, for an ICLR paper (single-column layout, 5.5 in text width).

Data: `learning_rates`, `batch_sizes`, and `accuracy[i][j]`: validation accuracy at learning rate i and batch size j.

Deliverable: the figure in the figures folder with its caption, as `hyperparameter-grid.png` and `hyperparameter-grid.pdf`.
