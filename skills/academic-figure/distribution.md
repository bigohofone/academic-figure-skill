# Distribution: how values are spread

Per-sample scores, per-problem accuracies, perplexities, lengths: many values per series, where the shape is the message. No value labels; the caption gives the numbers. Read with `SKILL.md`, whose rules all still apply.

- **Histogram**: raw values as touching bars, `ax.hist(values, bins=bins, color=color, linewidth=0)`; several series on one axis as outlines, `histtype="step", linewidth=1`. Counts per bin (e.g. accuracy intervals) as grouped bars with the bins in order on the x-axis, never horizontal bars. Horizontal gridlines only.
- **Box plot**: `box = ax.boxplot(values, positions=[x], widths=0.7, showfliers=False)` (the box fills 0.7 of its slot), filled with its series color lightened, `box["boxes"][0].set_facecolor(lighten(color))`, so the black median and the points on it stay readable; the style draws the black outline, whiskers, caps and median. With under ~30 points per box, show every point on top, jittered within the box, as the style's markers in black on white: `ax.plot(x + rng.uniform(-0.2, 0.2, len(values)), values, "o", color="black", markerfacecolor="white", linestyle="none")`. Horizontal gridlines only.
- **Violin**: `parts = ax.violinplot(values, positions=[x], widths=0.8, showextrema=False)`; fill the body with its series color lightened, at full opacity, with a black 0.75 pt edge (`body = parts["bodies"][0]`, `body.set_facecolor(lighten(color))`, `body.set_edgecolor("black")`, `body.set_linewidth(0.75)`, `body.set_alpha(1)`), and draw the median as a black 1 pt line across it; points as in box plots. Horizontal gridlines only.
