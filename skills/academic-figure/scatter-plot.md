# Scatter plot

Two quantities per item plotted against each other: accuracy against parameters, FLOPs or latency (a model comparison, often with a Pareto front), one point per sample to show a correlation, or a 2-D embedding (t-SNE, UMAP). Read with `DESIGN.md`, whose rules all still apply.

- **Many points** (samples, examples): `ax.scatter(x, y, s=5, color=color, edgecolors="none", alpha=0.5)`, dots the size of the design's markers, translucent so dense regions read darker. Gridlines in both directions. A fitted trend line, when the takeaway is the correlation: a 1 pt line in `darken(color)` and the coefficient in the caption (Pearson r or Spearman ρ, with n).
- **Few labelled points** (models compared on accuracy against cost): one marker per model, `ax.plot(x, y, "o", color=color)`, the design's hollow markers; colors as in DESIGN.md: with a proposed model, it is `colors["ours"]` and every other model `colors["neutral"]`; without one, `series-1`, or one color per model family when families are the message. Name each point next to it, `ax.annotate(name, (x, y), xytext=(3, 3), textcoords="offset points", fontsize=7, color=darken(color))`, moving a label to another side (`xytext=(-3, 3), ha="right"`) where `fit_height` reports an overlap. Cost axes (parameters, FLOPs, latency) spanning more than two orders of magnitude are log axes, labelled as in `line-chart.md`.
- **Pareto front**: connect the models on the front, in x order, with a 1 pt line in `colors["neutral"]` under the markers (`zorder=1`); dominated models stay as markers only.
- **Embedding** (t-SNE, UMAP): as many points, one color per class with a legend, square panel (aspect 1 : 1), ticks and tick labels hidden (`ax.set_xticks([])`, `ax.set_yticks([])`), no gridlines (`ax.grid(False)`) and no axis labels: the axes of an embedding mean nothing.
