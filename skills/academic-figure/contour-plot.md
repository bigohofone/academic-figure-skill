# Contour plot

A function of two continuous variables: a loss landscape, a decision boundary, an objective over two hyperparameters, a density in 2-D. Read with `DESIGN.md`, whose rules all still apply. A 3D surface plot is drawn as a contour plot instead: in print, a surface hides its own far side and its heights can't be read.

- Filled contours with lines on them, every line labelled with its level: `cs = ax.contourf(X, Y, Z, levels=10)` in the design's colormap (`Blues`; signed values: `RdBu_r` centered at 0, as in `heatmap.md`), then `lines = ax.contour(X, Y, Z, levels=cs.levels, colors="black", alpha=0.5)` at the design's `contour` width (0.5 pt; pass no `linewidths`), then `labels = ax.clabel(lines, fmt="%.2g")` (7 pt): one label per level, along a path where the lines are farthest apart and away from the panel edge, `ax.clabel(lines, fmt="%.2g", manual=positions)`; at half width use 6–8 levels so labels fit. Always a colorbar on the right, labelled with the quantity, so the reader can read the scale: `fig.colorbar(cs, ax=ax, label="Validation loss")`.
- Contour lines only, as many papers draw them: `lines = ax.contour(X, Y, Z, levels=...)`, colored by level from the design's colormap (drop its palest third so no line vanishes on white: `cmap=ListedColormap(plt.get_cmap("Blues")(np.linspace(0.35, 1, 256)))`), labelled the same way, with a colorbar for the line colors: `fig.colorbar(plt.cm.ScalarMappable(norm=Normalize(lo, hi), cmap=cmap), ax=ax, label=...)`.
- Contour lines that come as polylines with their levels rather than a grid (recovered from a paper's figure): draw each with `ax.plot(x, y, color=color, linewidth=rcParams["contour.linewidth"])` and write each level at its recorded label position, in the gap left for it, `ax.text(x, y, f"{level:.2g}", ha="center", va="center")`; where labels crowd at print size, keep the first of each group that would overlap.
- Panel aspect 1 : 1, no gridlines (`ax.grid(False)`), both axes labelled with their variables. A loss landscape's axes are the two directions in weight space, "Direction 1" and "Direction 2", with the trained solution at (0, 0).
- Points of interest on top: a minimum or the trained solution as a filled star in `colors["ours"]`, twice the design's marker size so it stands out on the lines, `ax.plot(x, y, "*", color=colors["ours"], markerfacecolor=colors["ours"], markersize=2 * rcParams["lines.markersize"], linestyle="none", label=...)`, a training trajectory as a data line in the series color with markers at its ends; named in a legend.
- A loss with a large range: contour its logarithm and say so in the caption ("contours of log loss").
